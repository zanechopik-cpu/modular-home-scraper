Modular Home Builder Finder

🏠 Find Modular Home Builders

Ask me about modular, manufactured, prefab, or tiny home builders in any city. I'll search the web and scrape company websites for verified contact information.

Press Enter to send. Searches take 5-7 minutes per city. Emails are only extracted from company websites.

🏠 How to Use

Example Questions

  • "Find modular home builders in Oklahoma City, Oklahoma"
  • "Show me manufactured homes in Denver, Colorado"
  • "Any prefab builders in Vancouver, British Columbia?"
  • "Search for tiny home builders in Austin, TX"

What I Do

I search the web for modular/manufactured/prefab home builders in your specified location, then visit each company's website to extract verified contact information.

Important Notes

  • Searches usually take 5-7 minutes per city
  • Email addresses are only extracted from company websites - never guessed
  • Results can be exported as CSV or JSON
  • Results are most accurate for major US and Canadian cities

Setup

This app requires an ANTHROPIC_API_KEY environment variable. Get yours at console.anthropic.com.
